About

I study how people actually make operational decisions, and how to design contracts, auctions and supply chain mechanisms that work with real behavior rather than against it. Most of my work uses controlled laboratory experiments, often paired with behavioral models, on topics such as procurement auctions, supply chain contracting, fairness and trust between trading partners, bargaining, and forecasting.

I am a Department Editor (Operations Management) at Management Science, co-editor of The Handbook of Behavioral Operations (Wiley, 2019), and co-director, with Gary Bolton, of the Laboratory for Behavioral Operations and Economics (LBOE) at UT Dallas. I have co-organized the annual Behavioral Operations Conference since 2006 and served as President of the INFORMS Behavioral Operations Management Section.

Before joining UT Dallas in 2012, I was on the faculty of the Smeal College of Business at Penn State, where I was the Calvin E. and Pamela T. Zimmerman Endowed Fellow and Professor of Supply Chain Management. I received my Ph.D. in Management Science from Penn State and my B.S. from UC Berkeley.
