Teaching Games

I have used games to teach supply chain management, procurement and negotiation for most of my career. I now build and maintain my own platform for running them.

myGames.live

myGames.live↗ (opens in a new tab) is a classroom platform for business courses with a growing library of market, auction and negotiation games. Students play from any browser, with nothing to install and no accounts to create. Results are scored automatically and sent to the instructor's gradebook when a round ends, so class time goes to the debrief.

  • Browse the game catalogue↗ (opens in a new tab)
  • Instructor login↗ (opens in a new tab)

The catalogue includes an implementation of Enno Siemsen's version of the beer distribution game. Instructors who want to use the games in their own courses are welcome to get in touch.
